About

I lead applied AI research at BeEmotion.ai, where the work centres on emotion-aware systems: models that read human affect and behaviour, and run efficiently on edge hardware.

My path runs from image processing and computational photography research at EPFL, through computer-vision engineering for imaging sensors, to leading data science and AI teams in industry. I care about taking research all the way to products that work reliably outside the lab.
